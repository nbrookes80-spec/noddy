# Fair Work law sources

The official version wins if sources conflict. For NSW-specific databases and AustLII search patterns, see also `../../whs-compliance/references/nsw-sources.md`.

## Legislation

| Source | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Federal Register of Legislation** | Authorised *Fair Work Act 2009* (Cth), *Fair Work Regulations 2009*, compilations by date, amending Acts (e.g. *Secure Jobs, Better Pay* 2022; *Protecting Worker Entitlements* 2023; *Closing Loopholes* 2023 and *Closing Loopholes No. 2* 2024) | https://www.legislation.gov.au |
| **AustLII** — Cth | Full-text search and point-in-time | https://www.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/cth/consol_act/ · OLEXI `au/legis/cth/consol_act`, `au/legis/cth/consol_reg` |
| **NSW Legislation** | *Industrial Relations Act 1996* (NSW) (state system), *Long Service Leave Act 1955* (NSW), *Anti-Discrimination Act 1977* (NSW), *Industrial Relations (Commonwealth Powers) Act 2009* (NSW) | https://legislation.nsw.gov.au · AustLII `au/legis/nsw/consol_act` |

## Awards, agreements, decisions

| Source | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Fair Work Commission** | Modern awards (current and historical), the annual wage review, enterprise agreement search, forms (F2 unfair dismissal, F8/F8C general protections, F72 stop bullying, plus the current sexual harassment forms ⚠), benchbooks, decisions | https://www.fwc.gov.au |
| **AustLII** — FWC | FWC single-member decisions `au/cases/cth/FWC`; Full Bench `au/cases/cth/FWCFB`; historical FWA/AIRC | https://www.austlii.edu.au/au/cases/cth/ |
| **Federal Court / FCFCOA / High Court** | Civil remedy proceedings, general protections, underpayments, appeals | AustLII `au/cases/cth/FCA`, `FCAFC`, `FedCFamC2G`, `HCA` · https://www.fedcourt.gov.au |
| **NSW Caselaw** | NSW Industrial Relations Commission / Industrial Court (state system), Local Court small claims | https://www.caselaw.nsw.gov.au |
| **Jade** | Citator, "cited by" | https://jade.io |

Leading authorities to look up and verify: *CFMMEU v Personnel Contracting Pty Ltd* [2022] HCA 1 and *ZG Operations Australia Pty Ltd v Jamsek* [2022] HCA 2 (employee vs contractor, for pre-26 Aug 2024 contracts); *WorkPac Pty Ltd v Rossato* [2021] HCA 23 (casuals, before the legislative changes); *Board of Bendigo Regional Institute of TAFE v Barclay* [2012] HCA 32 and *CFMEU v BHP Coal* [2014] HCA 41 (the reason for adverse action); FWC Full Bench authority on the s 387 criteria (search `au/cases/cth/FWCFB`).

## AustLII query examples

- `"s 394" AND "exceptional circumstances"` in `au/cases/cth/FWCFB`
- `"genuine redundancy" AND redeploy*` in `au/cases/cth/FWC`
- `"right to disconnect" OR "333M"` in `au/cases/cth/FWC`
- `"s 327A" OR "wage theft"` in `au/cases/cth/FCA`

## When sources are unreachable

Say which source failed, give the URL, and mark the item **unverified**. In a Claude Code cloud session, allow these hosts: `www.legislation.gov.au`, `www.austlii.edu.au`, `www.fwc.gov.au`, `www.fairwork.gov.au`, `www.caselaw.nsw.gov.au`, `legislation.nsw.gov.au`, `jade.io`.
