# Payroll tax sources

Order of authority: legislation > case law > Revenue NSW rulings (binding on the Chief Commissioner's practice, not on courts) > Revenue NSW guidance. For AustLII search patterns see `../../whs-compliance/references/nsw-sources.md`.

## Legislation

| Source | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **NSW Legislation** | *Payroll Tax Act 2007* (NSW); *Taxation Administration Act 1996* (NSW); any payroll tax relief or amendment Acts | https://legislation.nsw.gov.au |
| **AustLII** — NSW | Full-text search and point-in-time | https://www.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/nsw/consol_act/ · OLEXI `au/legis/nsw/consol_act` |
| Other states (harmonised provisions) | Vic *Payroll Tax Act 2007*; Qld *Payroll Tax Act 1971*; SA, Tas, ACT, NT Acts; WA *Pay-roll Tax Assessment Act 2002* | legislation.vic.gov.au · legislation.qld.gov.au · legislation.sa.gov.au · legislation.tas.gov.au · legislation.act.gov.au · legislation.nt.gov.au · legislation.wa.gov.au |

## Case law

| Source | Coverage | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **NSW Caselaw** | NSWCA, NSWSC, NCAT (Administrative and Equal Opportunity Division and Appeal Panel) | https://www.caselaw.nsw.gov.au |
| **AustLII** | `au/cases/nsw/NSWCA`, `NSWSC`, `NSWCATAD`; Vic `au/cases/vic/VSC`, `VSCA`, `VCAT`; Qld `au/cases/qld/QCA`, `QSC`, `QCAT` | https://www.austlii.edu.au |
| **Jade** | Citator | https://jade.io |

Authorities to look up and verify: *Thomas and Naaz Pty Ltd v Chief Commissioner of State Revenue* [2023] NSWCA 40 (medical centres / relevant contracts); *Commissioner of State Revenue v The Optical Superstore Pty Ltd* [2018] VSC 524 (Vic, relevant contracts). Search for later decisions: `"relevant contract" AND "payroll tax"` in `au/cases/nsw`.

| Source | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Revenue NSW** | Current rate and threshold; registration; lodgment and due dates; revenue rulings (PTA series); grouping and exclusion applications; contractor and medical-centre guidance; GP payroll tax concessions/amnesty ⚠; objections | https://www.revenue.nsw.gov.au/taxes-duties-levies-royalties/payroll-tax |
| **Other state revenue offices** | For interstate employers | sro.vic.gov.au · qro.qld.gov.au · revenuesa.sa.gov.au · sro.tas.gov.au · revenue.act.gov.au · treasury.nt.gov.au (Territory Revenue Office) · wa.gov.au (RevenueWA) |
| **NCAT** | Review of Revenue NSW decisions | https://ncat.nsw.gov.au |

## AustLII query examples

- `"payroll tax" AND "relevant contract" AND "medical"` in `au/cases/nsw`
- `"payroll tax" AND group* AND "exclu*"` in `au/cases/nsw/NSWCATAD`
- `"Payroll Tax Act 2007" AND "employment agent"` in `au/cases/nsw/NSWSC`

## When sources are unreachable

Say which source failed, give the URL, and mark the item **unverified**. In a Claude Code cloud session, allow these hosts: `www.revenue.nsw.gov.au`, `legislation.nsw.gov.au`, `www.caselaw.nsw.gov.au`, `www.austlii.edu.au`, `ncat.nsw.gov.au`, `jade.io`.
