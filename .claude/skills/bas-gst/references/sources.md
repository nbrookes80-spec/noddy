# BAS & GST sources

Order of authority: legislation > ATO public rulings (binding on the Commissioner) > case law on how the law applies > ATO web guidance (not binding). For AustLII search patterns and NSW databases, see also `../../whs-compliance/references/nsw-sources.md`.

## Legislation

| Source | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Federal Register of Legislation** | Authorised *A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999*, *GST Regulations 2019*, *Taxation Administration Act 1953* (incl. Schedule 1), *Fuel Tax Act 2006*, *Tax Agent Services Act 2009*; compilations by date | https://www.legislation.gov.au |
| **ATO Legal Database** | Acts and regulations, plus every public ruling (GSTR, GSTD, LCR, TR), practice statement (PS LA) and determination, with status history | https://www.ato.gov.au/law |
| **AustLII** — Cth | Full-text search | https://www.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/cth/consol_act/ · OLEXI `au/legis/cth/consol_act`, `au/legis/cth/consol_reg` |
| **NSW Legislation** / **Revenue NSW** | State taxes outside the BAS: *Payroll Tax Act 2007* (NSW), *Duties Act 1997* (NSW), *Land Tax Management Act 1956* (NSW), *Taxation Administration Act 1996* (NSW) | https://legislation.nsw.gov.au · https://www.revenue.nsw.gov.au |

## Case law and tribunal decisions

| Source | Where |
|---|---|
| High Court | AustLII `au/cases/cth/HCA` |
| Federal Court / Full Court | AustLII `au/cases/cth/FCA`, `au/cases/cth/FCAFC` |
| AAT (to 13 Oct 2024) / Administrative Review Tribunal (from 14 Oct 2024) | AustLII `au/cases/cth/AATA`; confirm the ART's database code |
| Jade (citator) | https://jade.io |

Leading GST cases to look up and verify: *Commissioner of Taxation v MBI Properties Pty Ltd* [2014] HCA 49 (going concern / leased residential premises and adjustments); *Travelex Ltd v Commissioner of Taxation* [2010] HCA 33 (GST-free supplies to non-residents); *Qantas Airways Ltd v Commissioner of Taxation* [2012] HCA 41 (supply and consideration on unused fares); *Commissioner of Taxation v Unit Trend Services Pty Ltd* [2013] HCA 16 (Division 165 anti-avoidance).

## ATO and regulator guidance (not law)

| Source | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **ATO — Business** | BAS labels and instructions, Simpler BAS, due dates and the lodgment calendar, GST registration, tax invoices, correcting GST errors, payment plans, penalty remission, director penalties | https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations |
| **ABR** | Check an ABN and its GST registration status (for input tax credits and no-ABN withholding) | https://abr.business.gov.au |
| **Tax Practitioners Board** | Check a registered tax or BAS agent; rules on who can provide BAS services | https://www.tpb.gov.au |
| **Australian Border Force** | Import GST and the deferred GST scheme | https://www.abf.gov.au |
| **business.gov.au** | Registrations, and state vs federal tax overview | https://business.gov.au |

## AustLII query examples

- `"going concern" AND "38-325"` in `au/cases/cth/AATA`
- `"margin scheme" AND "Division 75"` in `au/cases/cth/FCA`
- `"creditable purpose" AND "11-15"` in `au/cases/cth`

## When sources are unreachable

Say which source failed, give the URL, and mark the item **unverified**. In a Claude Code cloud session, allow these hosts: `www.legislation.gov.au`, `www.ato.gov.au`, `www.austlii.edu.au`, `abr.business.gov.au`, `www.tpb.gov.au`, `www.revenue.nsw.gov.au`, `legislation.nsw.gov.au`, `jade.io`.
