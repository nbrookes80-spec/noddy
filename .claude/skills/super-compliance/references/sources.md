# Superannuation compliance sources

Order of authority: legislation > ATO public rulings (binding on the Commissioner) > case law on how the law applies > ATO web guidance (not binding). For AustLII search patterns see `../../whs-compliance/references/nsw-sources.md`; for Fair Work and GST overlaps see the `fair-work` and `bas-gst` skills.

## Legislation

| Source | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Federal Register of Legislation** | *Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992*; *Superannuation Guarantee Charge Act 1992*; *Taxation Administration Act 1953* (Sch 1 penalties, Div 269 director penalties); *Income Tax Assessment Act 1997* Divs 290–293; *Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993* and Regulations; Payday Super amending Act ⚠ | https://www.legislation.gov.au |
| **ATO Legal Database** | Acts, SGR rulings (e.g. SGR 2009/2 on OTE), SGDs, practice statements (PS LA) with status history | https://www.ato.gov.au/law |
| **AustLII** — Cth | Full-text search | https://www.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/cth/consol_act/ · OLEXI `au/legis/cth/consol_act` |
| **NSW Legislation** / **Revenue NSW** | *Payroll Tax Act 2007* (NSW) — super as taxable wages | https://legislation.nsw.gov.au · https://www.revenue.nsw.gov.au |

## Case law

| Source | Where |
|---|---|
| High Court | AustLII `au/cases/cth/HCA` |
| Federal Court / Full Court | AustLII `au/cases/cth/FCA`, `au/cases/cth/FCAFC` |
| AAT (to 13 Oct 2024) / ART (from 14 Oct 2024) | AustLII `au/cases/cth/AATA`; confirm the ART's database code |
| Jade (citator) | https://jade.io |

Authorities to look up and verify: *CFMMEU v Personnel Contracting Pty Ltd* [2022] HCA 1; *ZG Operations Australia Pty Ltd v Jamsek* [2022] HCA 2; Full Federal Court decisions applying SGAA s 12(3) after those cases (search `"12(3)" AND "wholly or principally"` in `au/cases/cth/FCAFC`).

## AustLII query examples

- `"superannuation guarantee" AND "ordinary time earnings"` in `au/cases/cth/AATA`
- `"12(3)" AND "wholly or principally for the labour"` in `au/cases/cth/FCA`
- `"late payment offset" OR "23A"` in `au/cases/cth`

## When sources are unreachable

Say which source failed, give the URL, and mark the item **unverified**. In a Claude Code cloud session, allow these hosts: `www.ato.gov.au`, `www.legislation.gov.au`, `www.austlii.edu.au`, `www.fairwork.gov.au`, `www.revenue.nsw.gov.au`, `legislation.nsw.gov.au`, `jade.io`.
